Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026. Applies to the ReelTunes Android app.

Short version ReelTunes works on your device. Your audio files, playlists and settings never leave your phone. We do not have accounts, and we do not collect your name, email or location. Purchases are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat, and ads in the free version are served by Google AdMob. Promotional codes are checked by our own small server.

Delete your data There is no account to delete. Uninstalling ReelTunes removes its library, settings and folder permission from your phone. Your audio files stay in the folder you chose, and you can delete them with any file manager. To have purchase records removed, email contact.unfiltered.anshul@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Requests are handled within 30 days.

1. Who we are

ReelTunes is an Android app published by Anshul Rajpal. For any question about this policy or your data, contact contact.unfiltered.anshul@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your device

ReelTunes does not upload the following. It is stored only in the app's private storage and in the audios folder you choose:

  • Audio and video files you download or import, and any copy saved to your folder
  • Library entries: titles, durations, sources and playlists
  • App settings: theme, sort order, loop range, notification preferences, coin balance and earned perks
  • Purchase history and the purchase-summary PDF, when you choose to save one
  • The times of your subscription reminders, kept only so Android can reschedule them after a restart
  • The permission token for your audios folder, so the app can reopen it after a restart

The audio extraction runs entirely on the phone. No file is sent to a server for processing.

Internet is used only to fetch the media you ask for. When you paste a link, ReelTunes connects to that site and downloads the file you selected, then saves it to your folder. The site can see your IP address, as it would for any request from a browser. Nothing is uploaded back.

3. Data we do collect

Purchases

Pro subscriptions are sold through Google Play. Google is the merchant of record: it processes the payment, issues the official receipt and invoice, and handles cancellations and refunds. We never see your card details. ReelTunes never asks for your email address. RevenueCat, Inc. is used to validate purchases and keep your Pro status in sync. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, the products you bought, purchase and renewal dates, your country, platform and app version. It does not receive your name, email or payment details.

ReelTunes can create a purchase summary as a PDF on your device, using the plan, amount and dates already cached in the app. When you tap download, Android's system file picker asks where to save it. The PDF is never uploaded, emailed or sent to RevenueCat. It is a summary, not a tax invoice; Google Play's own receipt remains the official document.

A short purchase history (plan, amount, date, store) is kept only on this device so the PDF and the Pro screen can show it. It is cleared when you uninstall the app.

Subscription reminders

ReelTunes can remind you three days before Pro ends, one day before, and at the renewal minute. These reminders are scheduled locally with Android's alarm and notification services. The plan, price and end date needed for the reminder stay on your device; no reminder data is sent to us, to RevenueCat or to Google. You can turn reminders off in Settings. Exact timing on Android 12 and later needs the exact-alarm permission; without it the reminder still arrives, but Android may delay it a few minutes.

Promotional codes

When you redeem a promotional code, ReelTunes sends the code, a random device identifier and a random install identifier to our server, a Cloudflare Worker at reeltunes-codes.buildwise.workers.dev. The server stores the code, those identifiers and the time of redemption so the same code cannot be reused on the same device. It also keeps a salted hash of the request IP address for rate limiting and abuse checks. We do not receive your name, email, advertising ID or payment details through this request.

The device identifier is generated by the app at random. It is not your Android advertising ID, phone number or serial number. It is kept in the app's private settings and survives reinstalling ReelTunes, so a code cannot be redeemed twice on the same phone. Clearing the app's data or resetting the phone creates a new identifier. The install identifier changes whenever app data is cleared.

Redemption records are kept while the code is active and afterwards for fraud and abuse review. You can ask us to delete them using the contact below; we may keep the minimum needed to stop a code being farmed again. Redeeming a code does not add your files to any server.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with an ad, in order to serve and measure ads. Where required by law, a consent form is shown before personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings, Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android system settings. Pro users do not see ads.

Diagnostics

Error messages are shown on screen and written to the device log. They are not uploaded to us. Google Play may collect install, crash and performance data under Google's own privacy policy.

4. What we do not collect

  • No account, name, password or profile
  • No access to your contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone or precise location
  • No background reading of files you did not add to the app
  • No selling of personal data

5. Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications: to show the playback controls in the notification shade and on the lock screen, and to deliver optional subscription reminders. Playback works without it.
  • Exact alarms (Android 12+): optional, requested only for the subscription reminder at the renewal minute. If you decline, reminders are still scheduled but may be delayed.
  • Internet: to download the media from the link you paste, to check promotional codes, and for ads and purchase verification. Without a connection, links cannot be downloaded, new codes cannot be checked, and files already in your library play offline.
  • Foreground service (media playback): to keep audio playing when the app is in the background.
  • Folder access: granted by you through the Android folder picker, limited to the single folder you select. ReelTunes does not request broad storage permission.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the providers needed to run the app:

  • Google Play and RevenueCat, for purchases and Pro status
  • Google AdMob, for ads in the free version

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Uninstalling ReelTunes deletes the app's library, settings and permissions from the device. Audio files remain in your own folder.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as required for billing, tax and fraud prevention. The local purchase history and reminder schedule are deleted when the app is uninstalled.
  • A purchase-summary PDF you saved is yours; it remains in the location you chose until you delete it.
  • To stop ad personalisation, use the consent options in Settings or reset your advertising ID in Android.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to processing. Because ReelTunes has no accounts, most data is already only on your device. For anything held by our providers, contact us and we will help or forward the request. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

ReelTunes is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Security

Data sent to our providers uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Files and settings on your phone are protected by Android's app sandbox and your device lock. No system is completely secure, so keep your device protected.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

12. Contact

Questions, data requests or complaints: contact.unfiltered.anshul@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.
